# Starting questions (ask only those that have no answer yet)

## Platform and environment
- Which phone: Android or iPhone? Is a second platform needed in the foreseeable future?
- Do you listen in the car (Android Auto / CarPlay), through a Bluetooth headset?

## Content
- Roughly how many books and albums, and what total size? The typical size of one MP3?
- Is a book one large file or many chapter files? Does the order matter, and where
  does it come from (the order of messages in the channel, the file name, the tag number)?
- Are the channels public, private or both? Are they your own?
- Is there anything besides audio in the channels (covers, descriptions) worth showing?

## Listening
- What is a must for books: speed, sleep timer, 15/30 s skip, chapters, bookmarks?
- What is a must for music: shuffle, repeat, queue?
- Is listening without a network needed (books downloaded in advance)? How much
  space are you ready to give?

## Synchronization
- Do you listen on several devices? Is position sync between them needed in the MVP?

## Scope
- Is this a personal tool or some day for other people (it affects store
  publishing, authorization, legal questions)?
- What will be the sign that "the MVP worked"?
